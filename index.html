Which Formative settings keep my test secure?

Answer two or three questions about what students should see after they submit, and get the exact assign settings to use. Below the finder: how the settings work together, a side‑by‑side table, and the misunderstandings that trip most of us up.

The one setting that decides whether students can get back in: After submission. The entry password and the Secure Exam Browser only protect the test while students are taking it. Once a student submits, Keep visible lets them reopen it from any device, anywhere. Make hidden locks it.

Find your settings

Settings are chosen per class section in the Assign window (click the arrow next to the class name), and you can change them at any time.

How the settings stack up over a test’s life

Each setting only works during one stage. Most confusion comes from expecting a test‑day setting to keep working after students submit.

1

Before they start

  • Entry password keeps students out until you give it to them (case sensitive).
  • Schedule open/close times controls when the test is available.
  • Assign to individual students controls who gets it at all.
2

While they take it

  • Secure Exam Browser blocks other tabs, apps, printing and screen capture until they submit.
  • Time limit and random question order.
  • Return scores: Instantly turns on Check answer for auto‑graded questions during the test.
3

After they submit

  • After submission decides whether they can reopen it.
  • Return scores and Return correct answers decide what they see.
  • Entry password and Secure Exam Browser no longer apply.
4

After you close it

  • Closing only stops new responses.
  • If a score or answers are set to return, the test stays on student dashboards.
  • If nothing is set to return, it disappears from dashboards.

Common misunderstandings

Test‑day protections for any scenario

These protect the test while students take it. None of them affect what students can see afterward.

Entry password

Prevents early peeking and someone logging in from home. Stops applying to each student once they submit.

Secure Exam Browser

Respondus LockDown Browser (our add‑on). Add a quit password for emergencies, and turn on auto‑pause after a swipe‑away attempt.

Restrict to single device

Available with LockDown Browser. Stops a second login on another device from taking the test for the student.

Random order or versions

Randomize question order, or duplicate the test, make small changes, and assign versions to different students. Makes a copied question bank less useful.

Avoid “Instantly” on tests

Return scores: Instantly lets students check auto‑graded answers mid‑test. Save it for practice.

Preview as student

In the Assign window, use Preview as student to confirm your settings behave the way you expect before assigning.

In‑class review, step by step

For when you want students to go over the test with you but not take it home. Because settings are per class, opening it for one section doesn’t open it for the others.

  1. Assign with After submission: Make hidden.Set Return scores to After student submits or When closed. Students see a score but can’t open the test.
  2. Close the activity once make‑ups are done.This stops new responses. With Make hidden still on, the content stays locked.
  3. At the start of the review, open that section only.Change After submission to Keep visible (no edits allowed) and Return correct answers to When closed. Select Update.
  4. Review with phones away.Nothing in Formative can block screenshots or phone photos during this window, and students can open it on any device.
  5. Before the bell, lock it again.Change After submission back to Make hidden and select Update. Correct answers switch off automatically. Repeat steps 3–5 for each section.
Most secure alternative: skip steps 3–5 and lead the review from your own screen. Project the questions from your teacher view (hide student names) while students look at their score. No access is ever opened.
